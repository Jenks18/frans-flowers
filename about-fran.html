Meet Fran

Francine "Fran" Miller founded Fran's Flowers in 1995 with a simple dream: to spread joy and beauty through flowers in the heart of San Francisco's Mission District.

With over 30 years of experience in floral design, Fran learned her craft from her mother, who was a passionate gardener and florist herself. What started as a small shop with a handful of flowers has grown into a beloved community staple.

Every arrangement that leaves our shop carries Fran's personal touch—a commitment to quality, creativity, and making every occasion special. Whether it's a wedding, a birthday, or just because someone needs a smile, Fran believes flowers have the power to connect us.

Our Mission

Quality First

We source the finest, freshest flowers available. Every stem is hand-selected to ensure your arrangement is beautiful and long-lasting.

Community Focused

10% of every sale goes directly to fighting homelessness in the Bay Area. Buying flowers from Fran's means supporting real change in our community.

Creativity & Connection

We don't just make arrangements—we create moments. Our team takes time to understand your needs and craft something truly special.

Sustainability

We're committed to environmentally responsible practices, from sourcing to packaging. Beautiful flowers shouldn't come at the cost of our planet.

A Family Business

Over the years, Fran has built a team of passionate florists who share her vision. Each member of our team is dedicated to bringing happiness to our customers and supporting our community.

What makes Fran's special isn't just the flowers—it's the people. When you walk through our doors, you're not just a customer. You're part of the Fran's Flowers family.
